Distributor Search — Step by Step

Identification phase only — do not apply or contact without Matthew's approval
Steps completed 0 / 12
Step 1 Duplicate Check — Before Anything Else
✓
Search the distributor name in ClickUp CRM AND the Distributor Main Google Sheet
STOP if they already exist in either — mark as Already on ClickUp and move to the next distributor. Only proceed if they are not listed anywhere.
Step 2 Website Review
✓
Open the website — check if it looks B2B or consumer-facing, and whether pricing is publicly visible
DISQUALIFY if prices are clearly shown on product pages without logging in. OK if it says "Sign in to view pricing" or "Contact us for a quote."
✓
Confirm they carry multiple brands and are not a single manufacturer or consumer retailer
DISQUALIFY if single brand, manufacturer, or consumer retailer. STRONG SIGNAL if they have flyers, catalogues, and active brand partnerships across many brands.
✓
Find and note the credit application or account setup link — look for: Credit Application, Become a Dealer, Apply for Account, Open Terms, or Wholesale Inquiry
DISQUALIFY if the only option is a Google/email login with no business fields. OK if it's a "call to apply," "submit a request," or a form asking for EIN and trade references. Record the link — do NOT fill it in.
✓
Note their address, branch locator, or location map — you'll need this for the next step
TIP Check the footer, About Us, Contact Us, or Locations pages.
Step 3 Location Verification — The FBI Method
✓
Paste their address into Google Search — confirm it returns their own name, listing, or warehouse images
RED FLAG if it returns a different company, a residential area, or an unrelated office. Investigate before disqualifying — they may have moved.
✓
Open Google Maps and Street View — confirm the building looks like a warehouse, distribution center, or industrial facility
GOOD Large building, loading docks, trucks, company signage. ACCEPTABLE Plain industrial building with no signage — still may be legitimate. If multiple locations exist, check 2–3 of them. If still unclear, ask AI: "Is [Company Name] a legitimate distributor? Do they deliver nationwide?"
Step 4 Geographic Suitability
✓
Confirm they can deliver to Wisconsin — national distributor OR regional within 1–2 states of WI
KEEP National, or regional in WI, MN, IL, IA, MI, IN, OH.
TO REVIEW Regional but nearest location is within 1–2 states — note the location.
DISQUALIFY Strictly regional to CA, New England, or Deep South only.
NOTE Single location is NOT a disqualifier if they ship nationally — confirm via website or AI.
Step 5 Dealer Locator Check (If Applicable)
✓
If a "Find a Dealer" or "Authorized Dealer" section exists — check whether dealers are diverse or all the same company
GOOD Many different companies and store types — open distribution, we have a chance.
DISQUALIFY All dealers are the same company (e.g. all Fastenal) — exclusive arrangement.
FLAG Small number of dealers, all same type — flag for Logan or Matthew rather than disqualifying. Skip this step entirely if no dealer locator exists.
Step 6 AI & SpyFu Research (When Needed)
✓
Use AI to confirm anything still unclear — national vs regional, delivery scope, legitimacy, employee count
PROMPTS "Is [Company] a national or regional distributor?" / "Do they deliver to Wisconsin?" / "Is [Company] a legitimate wholesale distributor or a retailer?" Always cross-check AI results with your own website review — don't rely on AI alone.
✓
Run the distributor URL through SpyFu — scroll to competitor websites and note any that could also be distributors worth researching
NOTE SpyFu is a supplemental discovery tool, not a primary one. Use it to find more leads, not to verify the current distributor.
Step 7 Log in ClickUp
✓
Add to ClickUp with: company name, website URL, application link, product categories, brief notes, correct status, and date last touched updated
Statuses: Accounts to Apply For — all checks passed. To Review — unsure or one concern. On Hold — needs verification or bank references required (note why). Disqualified — always note the reason clearly.
NEVER submit any application, fill in any form, or contact any distributor at this stage.
✦ Search complete. Distributor is fully researched and logged in ClickUp.
